## In short

I'm a technical founder and venture builder with 25 years of experience across the United States and Thailand. The reputation I've earned along the way is a specific one: I'm the guy who can solve the impossibility of business requirements for the team.

Today I'm CEO and Co-Founder of QUEST EDTECH in Bangkok — and, since May 2026, in Seoul — Chief Strategy Officer for CUBIKA / Digital Dialogue and for SSL Logistics, Entrepreneur in Residence at Whatnot Startup Studio, founder of duPhonics, and an advisor to Digital Bridge Thailand. In 2025 I was selected as a K-Scouter, scouting and coaching Southeast Asian companies into Korea's startup programmes with Nautilus Investment.

I work in Thai and English, from Bangkok, and spend a good part of the year in Seoul and Singapore.

## Where I come from

I grew up in Texas. Richland College first (International Business, 1996–98), then The University of Texas at Dallas for a BS in Information System Management (1998–2003).

In 1999, as a junior, I joined the founding team of StockSmart.com — one of the world's first fintech startups. I helped secure the business partnership behind British Telecom's first 3G implementation, and helped build the world's first real-time fintech app with NTT DoCoMo's FOMA network and Credit Suisse. That internship was the pivotal moment: it set the path for everything since.

I spent the next eight years in Dallas startups building mobile applications and games while the app economy was being invented, and picked up an Oracle DBA certification (2003) along the way. In 2006 I coordinated a project between CommunityMobile and Thailand's Land Development Department — a 12-terabyte aerial-photography database made searchable online, with web-services GIS on top. In 2007 I moved to the other side of the table as a Quantitative Analyst and Qualitative Reporter at Morning Star Research, covering the Australian Stock Exchange.

In 2012 I found my passion for education and moved to Thailand. I've been building there ever since.

## What I build

### QUEST EDTECH — CEO & Co-Founder, 2018 →

QUEST began at the Edtech Hackathon 2018 in Bangkok and was the only Thai startup among the top finalists of Project Alpha by SeedPlus in Singapore that year. We built a real-time platform for digital literacy — coding and language skills together — won US$80,000 in grants from Depa and the TED Fund for a blockchain-backed workforce-management application, and closed a seed round led by Reapra with angel investors in February 2020.

Products over the years: a full-stack coding curriculum, the Coding Co-Pilot Tutor (2024), GED, IGCSE and IELTS preparation, REACH LearnSpace, and duPhonics. In 2025 QUEST became an AI consulting practice — LLM localisation for Thai and Southeast Asian languages, agentic AI systems, and an agentic HR proof of concept — while the products kept running. In May 2026 I incorporated QUEST EDTECH Korea in Seoul as an application-software company.

### duPhonics — Founder, 2020 →

A telenanny platform for life literacy, started in the first months of the pandemic. Children learn inside a real-time metaverse where a teacher is present and generative AI composes the curriculum — images, video and audio — as the lesson goes. It's built for Gen Alpha, who are VR/XR natives; our pitch persona is Anna, born after 2010, who has never known a world without a screen she can step into.

duPhonics went through the APTG 5G Accelerator (2022), showed at Smart City Expo 2022 through the TED Fund, at EdTech Asia in 2023 and 2024, on stage at NextRise 2024 Seoul (sponsored by DPIT, Ministry of Commerce of Thailand), and at TECHBITE 5.0's Investment & Demo Day in 2024.

### QuestLanguage — Co-Founder, 2013 →

My first venture in Thailand, and where I still teach. IELTS, SAT and TOEFL preparation for secondary students with a 100% success rate into the top three international undergraduate programmes in Thailand. I was head coach, teacher and chief software engineer: we ran live classes on BigBlueButton with our own QUEST STUDY LMS behind them, and proved the model with students in the US, UK, Canada, Australia and New Zealand before online school was normal.

### REACH LearnSpace — 2020

A real-time, multi-user learning space with a shared whiteboard — a Zoom alternative built for education. Y Combinator Startup School, 2020. The 2020 press on our seed round described it as QUEST's online conferencing and education product for Thai SMEs.

## How I work

- **Problem first, technology second.** I pick an underserved problem — Gen Alpha literacy, carbon data, tariff-shocked logistics — and then reach for the newest technology that actually solves it.
- **AI-first, not AI as an add-on.** Integrate AI into the workflow from day one. Make AI your errand bot, not the other way around.
- **Speed to revenue.** The smallest MVP that can test the market and earn. Explore, validate, build, grow.
- **Fundraising-ready by design.** Structure the business the way investors will read it: unit economics, a traction narrative, a defensible story.
- **Experience over theory.** I've raised, pitched, won and lost in real rooms. I coach from that, not from a framework.
- **Cross-border by default.** Thai companies should be landing in Korea, Japan and Singapore; corporates should be open-innovating with startups.
- **Pay it forward.** Mentoring at e27, BASCII and Digital Bridge is part of the job, not charity.
